Team news

Fofana, Palestra and Lavia are all on the bench, so all three are fit and dropped by choice, not injury.

Pep Chavarria is not in the matchday squad. Reece James, Emegha and Gittens are absent as expected.

Caicedo captains the side while James is out.

The bench: Penders, Fofana, Palestra, Barco, Lavia, Nicoll-Jazuli, Estêvão, Quenda, Welbeck.

What changed from the projection

Three changes from this page's projection. Josh Acheampong starts instead of Fofana, Pedro Neto instead of Palestra, and Jordan Henderson instead of Lavia.

The Lavia call was wrong. We read James's injury as an opening for Lavia beside Caicedo, but Alonso went with Henderson, who started last weekend.

Eight of eleven correct, including the shape, the back-line spine of Lacroix and Colwill, Hato at left wing-back, and the front three of Palmer, Rogers and João Pedro.

Per the club, Alonso made one change from last weekend's XI: Hato comes in on the left for Chavarria.

The tactical read

Still a back three. Acheampong, Lacroix and Colwill play behind Neto and Hato at wing-back, with Palmer and Rogers behind João Pedro.

Neto at right wing-back is the attacking option. He is a winger playing deeper, where Palestra would have been a natural full-back, so expect Chelsea to push higher down the right.

Henderson brings experience and passing range beside Caicedo, rather than the more defensive Lavia pairing we projected.

What to watch

Henderson's audition beside Caicedo. With James out until around the new year, this is the first signal of who Alonso trusts in that role.

Neto against Mykolenko down Everton's left. Neto's defending will be tested in his deeper role.

Acheampong over Fofana. It is a vote of confidence in the 20-year-old at right centre-back.
