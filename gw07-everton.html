Team news

Reece James is out for around three months after minor knee surgery, following the injury he picked up in training. Alonso said the operation went well. Sky Sports puts his return in 2027, and Alonso expects him back some weeks either side of the January window.

Emmanuel Emegha is ruled out of Everton. Alonso said he "felt a little thing" in training on Thursday. He will train next week, may get Under-21 minutes first, and is still waiting for his competitive debut.

Jamie Gittens is still a few weeks away with the injury from England Under-21 duty.

No other new problems were raised. Caicedo, Joao Pedro, Palmer and Palestra carry no fresh concern.

What changed from the projection

No change to the XI after the press conference. The projection already had Roméo Lavia in for James, and Alonso's news confirms that call rather than overturning it.

One correction to this page's team news. Earlier this week it said Emegha was back in training and could make the squad. He is now out of this match with a fresh knock.

James moves from doubt to long-term absence. The earlier page called him a week-by-week assessment with Tottenham as a likely return. That was too optimistic.

The tactical read

Still the 3-4-2-1. Fofana, Lacroix and Colwill at the back, with Palestra and Hato as wing-backs, and Palmer and Rogers behind Joao Pedro.

Lavia beside Caicedo is the softest pick. It is a more defensive pairing than Caicedo with James. Enzo Fernández or Andrey Santos are the alternatives if Alonso wants more passing range.

What to watch

Lavia's audition beside Caicedo. With James gone until around the new year, this is the start of a long run for whoever partners Caicedo. A strong game makes Lavia the default pick.

Who wears the armband. Alonso pointed to Caicedo and Colwill as leaders while James is out.

The early kick-off. A 12:30 UK start leaves little recovery time, so watch for rotation at wing-back, with Welbeck the only fit senior cover up front.
