Team news

The official sheet is in, and all three fitness doubts went against Chelsea. João Pedro, Reece James and Moisés Caicedo are all absent. Alonso said on Thursday that nobody was ruled out and that every call would wait for Friday's final session; that session evidently went badly for the lot of them. Marco Palestra and Emmanuel Emegha are also listed out.

Levi Colwill captains the side in James's absence, from left centre-back.

Jordan Henderson starts, and it is his first competitive appearance for Chelsea. The wrist problem that clouded his availability all week is behind him. He makes that debut against Brentford, the club he left this summer after 34 appearances for them last season — the kind of fixture symmetry nobody scripts.

The bench is Penders, Acheampong, Gusto, Hato, Lavia, Nicoll-Jazuli, Gittens, Estêvão and Quenda. That is a deep attacking bench: Estêvão, Gittens and Quenda are all available to change the game, and Lavia and Hato give Alonso an orthodox midfield and left-side reset if the improvised setup does not hold.

Brentford name Kelleher; Kayode, Ajer, Schuster, Lewis-Potter; Janelt, Yarmoliuk, Damsgaard, Anthony, Schade; Thiago, with Balcombe, Hickey, Henry, Ouattara, Wilson, Carvalho, Sangaré, Diouf and Fredrick among the substitutes. Nathan Collins and Sepp van den Berg are both missing from the back line, as flagged all week.

What changed from the projection

This morning's contested call was the right one, and it deserves its moment. The 8:03 AM pass changed the projection on the strength of two fresh matchday previews and flagged itself as "the least secure call on the page" — Maxence Lacroix into the back three, Malo Gusto out. The official sheet has exactly that: Lacroix starts, Gusto is a substitute. The page backed the freshest evidence over the oldest, said out loud which half of the call was shakier, and got both halves right.

Seven of the eleven were called correctly — Martínez; Fofana, Lacroix, Colwill; Neto; Palmer and Rogers. The spine this page described all week as the secure part of the projection was, in fact, the secure part.

Four were wrong, and they are all the same story: the doubts resolved against us. João Pedro is out and Danny Welbeck leads the line — the named fallback, exactly as identified, just deployed sooner than hoped. Reece James is out rather than rested, and Jordan Henderson takes a midfield slot. Romeo Lavia does not start; Valentin Barco is in alongside Henderson. Jorrel Hato is benched and Pep Chavarría starts at left wing-back.

The honest miss is Barco, who was not on this page's radar at all. Yesterday's projection graded João Pedro as the likeliest name to drop and then softened that after Alonso's presser; the original read was better than the revision. And where this page hedged James as "precautionary management, not an injury" and treated him as likely to start, he is out of the squad entirely.

The tactical read

It is the 3-4-2-1 this page projected, with a heavily improvised middle. Fofana, Lacroix and Colwill form the back three; Neto and Chavarría are the wing-backs; Palmer and Rogers tuck in behind Welbeck. The shape held even as four of the personnel changed, which says the structure is Alonso's, not the players'.

The midfield two is the improvisation, and it is a real one. Valentin Barco is a left wing-back by trade — left-footed, low centre of gravity, known for his delivery — and he is being asked to play centrally alongside Henderson. With James, Caicedo and Lavia all unavailable and Enzo not in the side, Alonso has run out of natural central midfielders and has solved it by moving a full-back inside rather than by dropping Lavia in off an injury.

Chavarría's start is a straight swap of profile on the left. Hato is the safer, more positionally conservative option; Chavarría was signed from Rayo Vallecano as the specialist attacking replacement for Cucurella, with stamina and a reliable cross. Away from home with a makeshift midfield in front of him, how high he pushes is a live question.

Welbeck against a patched Brentford back line is the matchup Alonso has chosen. He presses intelligently and holds the ball up — useful when the midfield behind him is unfamiliar and may need the ball to stick. Brentford are without Collins and van den Berg, so both defences are improvised, and the long throw into a crowded box remains Brentford's signature weapon against a back three that is at least tall and quick.

What to watch

Jordan Henderson's first competitive Chelsea appearance, against the club he left in the summer. He is 36, he is walking straight into an improvised midfield, and he is the senior voice in a side captained by Colwill rather than James.

Barco in central midfield. A left wing-back playing in the middle, away from home, against a direct side that attacks second balls. This is the single most fragile part of the setup, and the bench holds Lavia if it needs undoing.

Whether the depleted spine changes how high Chelsea press. Three of the four senior midfield options are unavailable. Watch whether Alonso keeps the front five aggressive or asks Palmer and Rogers to do more defensive work than usual to protect the two behind them.
