The situation

A new era opening in the least forgiving place

Chelsea finished 10th last season, went trophyless after losing the FA Cup final to Manchester City, burned through two managers (Enzo Maresca in January, Liam Rosenior in April), and are without European football for the first time in nine years. Xabi Alonso arrived on 1 July on a four-year deal after leaving Real Madrid in January, and inherits a squad that has already absorbed more than £220m (about $295m) of new spending this summer.

The opener is a west London derby, away, on a Monday night, in front of a full Craven Cottage — Sky's first Monday Night Football of the season. There is no soft landing here.

What it means: the fixture is a genuine read on whether Alonso's back-three model has bedded in during a five-match Asia-Pacific pre-season, with several starters only reporting late after the World Cup. Expect a cautious, control-first Chelsea rather than the free-flowing version people remember from Leverkusen.

Depth and open questions

What could change this XI

Genuine competition

Attack: Liam Delap (9) and Emanuel Emegha — the Strasbourg captain whose move was pre-agreed back in September 2025 — both press the striker spot; Estêvão (41), Jamie Gittens (11), Pedro Neto (7) and new arrival Geovany Quenda give Alonso five more forward options. Alejandro Garnacho has left on loan.

Midfield: Romeo Lavia (45) and Dário Essugo (14) behind Caicedo and Enzo. Andrey Santos was sold to Manchester United for around £50m, which thinned this group more than expected.

Defence: Malo Gusto, Trevoh Chalobah (23), Tosin Adarabioyo (4), Mamadou Sarr (19), Benoît Badiashile, Wesley Fofana (29), Aaron Anselmino (30). Several are expected to leave — Lacroix's arrival makes that queue longer, not shorter.

The three things to watch on the night: (1) does Alonso actually go with a back three, or hedge to a four; (2) is Enzo Fernández in the XI at all, or already halfway to Madrid; (3) how sharp are the late World Cup returnees — James, Gusto, Chalobah and Enzo all reported to pre-season after the others.
