Team news

Caicedo starts and captains the side. He was listed as a doubt, but he is fit. Gusto was also a doubt and is on the bench. Hato is not in the matchday squad, which suggests he did not recover in time.

Lacroix and João Pedro are absent, as expected. Henderson is not in the squad either.

Chavarría and Emegha are on the bench. Our injury list had both of them out long-term, so they have returned sooner than we recorded. Quenda and Welbeck are also among the substitutes.

What changed from the projection

The projection got eight of the eleven right. That includes the whole back three: Penders, Acheampong, Fofana and Colwill. It was also right about Caicedo, Neto, Rogers and Palmer.

We missed three players. Estêvão starts at last, ahead of Quenda. Lavia takes Henderson's place in midfield. Palestra comes in at right wing-back, which moves Neto to the left side to cover for Hato.

The tactical read

ESPN's data feed lists a 3-4-2-1. The back three is Acheampong, Fofana and Colwill. Palestra plays wide on the right and Neto on the left, with Caicedo and Lavia in the middle.

The sources disagree about who leads the line. ESPN puts Palmer in the central attacking slot, between Estêvão and Rogers. SB Nation lists Rogers as the striker, with Palmer behind him. SB Nation also says the side could line up with four at the back, with Palestra and Acheampong as full-backs.

What to watch

Estêvão's first league start of the season. Watch how often he gets the ball isolated against his full-back on the right.

Back three or back four. Watch whether Palestra pushes up as a wing-back or stays back as a full-back. That tells you which shape Alonso has picked.

Lavia and Caicedo together. It is a more mobile midfield than Henderson would have given them.
