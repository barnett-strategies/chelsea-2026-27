Team news

Moisés Caicedo is fit. Alonso confirmed at Thursday's press conference that he has trained this week and "is going to be okay" for Friday, so the doubt is gone.

Wesley Fofana is available. Alonso said the matter of his unauthorised interview is closed.

Still out: João Pedro and Lacroix, who is closer but "not for this game". Hato is still listed as a doubt by Sports Mole. James was out in the earlier previews, and nothing has changed that.

Martínez is expected to keep the gloves. Alonso did not name his keeper.

What changed from the projection

One change from the first projection: Caicedo in for Henderson. The first version only had Henderson in because Caicedo was a doubt. Thursday's fitness news settles that, so he goes back alongside Lavia, the same pairing that started the last league game.

Neto on the right is still a guess. The previews prefer him to Estêvão, but the presser said nothing on it. Nobody has published an XI since the presser.

The tactical read

A 4-2-3-1 again, with Caicedo and Lavia as the double pivot.

Rogers, Palmer and Neto sit behind Emegha, with João Pedro still out. Fofana and Colwill are expected at centre-back, with Lacroix still missing.

What to watch

Estêvão or Neto on the right. This is the selection most likely to differ from the official team sheet.

Who leads the line. With João Pedro still out, Emegha is the projection, but it is not settled.
