Team news

Colwill is on the bench, not in the XI, so he is fit but not starting. Emegha and Estêvão are also among the substitutes.

Still out: João Pedro, Lacroix, Reece James and Hato are all missing from the squad, as the previews said.

Caicedo starts and captains the side, which settles the fitness doubt from the Leeds game.

Bench: Penders, Colwill, Gusto, Nicoll-Jazuli, Palestra, Estêvão, Barco, Quenda, Emegha.

What changed from the projection

Three changes from our projection. Welbeck leads the line instead of Emegha. Henderson comes into midfield. Colwill drops to the bench, and Caicedo reportedly moves back to centre-back to cover him.

What we got right: the 4-2-3-1, Martínez in goal, Acheampong, Fofana and Chavarría in the back four, Lavia in the pivot, and Neto, Palmer and Rogers behind the striker. Our call of Neto over Estêvão on the right was correct.

What we missed: we called the striker and centre-back spots wrong. The previews and the presser gave no hint that Welbeck or Henderson would start.

The tactical read

A 4-2-3-1, as projected, but with a different spine.

Caicedo at centre-back next to Fofana is the headline. That is the Yahoo/SB Nation team sheet. ESPN's graphic draws him in midfield with Lavia at the back, so treat his exact role as reported, not settled. Either way, Henderson and Lavia (or Caicedo) form the pivot.

Welbeck is an experienced target man who holds the ball up and runs the channels. Expect Palmer and Rogers to play off him rather than in behind.

What to watch

Where Caicedo actually lines up. Watch the first few build-up phases to see whether he drops between the centre-backs or into the back line.

Welbeck against Forest's back three. Watch whether he can pin Murillo and Milenković and bring Palmer into play.
