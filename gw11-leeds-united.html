Team news

Caicedo starts. He was cleared at Friday's presser after Ecuador managed his minutes over the break.

Martínez is back in goal, with Penders on the bench. Alonso kept that choice to himself on Friday, and it is now made.

Absent from the twenty, as the presser said: João Pedro, Lacroix, Hato and James. Gittens is not in the squad either.

Bench: Penders, Subuloye, Gusto, Palestra, Barco, Henderson, Neto, Quenda, Welbeck.

What changed from the projection

Four changes from the projection. Acheampong replaces Palestra at right-back. Estêvão comes in on the right and Henderson drops to the bench. Emmanuel Emegha leads the line ahead of Pedro Neto, his first Premier League start.

What we got right: seven of eleven, including the two calls that were genuinely open on Friday, Martínez over Penders and Caicedo starting. The back four shape, Colwill, Fofana and Chavarría all held.

What we got wrong: we carried the last league XI forward and left Estêvão out, even though both published previews brought him in. They were right and we were not. Nobody we read had Emegha starting.

The tactical read

A 4-2-3-1, as ESPN's team sheet lays it out. Caicedo and Lavia are the double pivot, and Henderson's place goes to an attacker rather than a third midfielder.

Estêvão, Palmer and Rogers sit behind Emegha. With João Pedro out, Alonso picked a true number nine over playing Neto through the middle.

Acheampong at right-back rather than in a back three. That is the Evening Standard's personnel in Premier League News Now's shape.

What to watch

Emegha's first league start. How he links with Palmer and Rogers as a target man, a different look from Neto's running.

Caicedo's minutes. He is cleared, but if he comes off early, Henderson and Barco are on the bench.

Martínez's first league start since the jaw fracture, and what it says about Alonso's number one.
