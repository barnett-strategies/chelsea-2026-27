Team news

Emi Martínez is fit again. He has been out since jaw surgery in October, and Argentina picked him for a friendly this break. That makes goalkeeper a real choice between him and Mike Penders, who has kept the shirt.

Moisés Caicedo is a fitness doubt. He told Ecuadorian reporters he is tired and has some problems. Ecuador play Bolivia on 17 Nov, so he faces a long flight and only about three days before kickoff.

Out: Reece James (knee, mid-January), Maxence Lacroix (hamstring, January) and Jamie Gittens (ankle). João Pedro, Jorrel Hato and Josh Acheampong are all listed as possible returns for Leeds. Alonso has said he will give a fuller update before the game.

The tactical read

This is a guess. It repeats the XI that started the last league match: a back four, Caicedo, Henderson and Lavia in midfield, Palmer and Rogers behind Pedro Neto up front.

Pre-match predictions disagree on the shape. At least one preview has Alonso going back to a back three, with Acheampong returning and Danny Welbeck up front. If João Pedro is passed fit, he would probably lead the line instead.

What to watch

Martínez or Penders. It is the first selection call since Martínez was cleared, so it shows who Alonso sees as his number one.

Caicedo after the trip home from Ecuador. Watch whether he starts. If he does not, Barco, on the bench last time, is the obvious midfield alternative.

Who plays up front. Neto, Welbeck or a returning João Pedro. The Friday press conference should settle it.
