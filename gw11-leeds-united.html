Team news

Alonso has cleared Moisés Caicedo. At his Friday press conference he said Caicedo "is available". Ecuador cut his minutes over the break to protect him, and the earlier doubt is over.

Emi Martínez is fit after the jaw fracture he suffered on 17 October, and he played a full international for Argentina in a mask. Mike Penders is also available. Alonso did not say which of them starts.

Out: João Pedro (thigh, "not yet" training with the squad, game by game), Maxence Lacroix (hamstring), Jorrel Hato (back) and Reece James (knee, still weeks away). Jamie Gittens is back in team training, but Alonso says he needs more time.

Josh Acheampong is fit again after missing the last match with a minor problem. None of Friday's reports mentions Aaron Anselmino.

The tactical read

This is a guess. It repeats the XI that started the last league match, with one change: Martínez in goal. That leaves a back four, Caicedo, Henderson and Lavia in midfield, and Palmer and Rogers behind Pedro Neto.

The previews disagree on the shape. The Evening Standard has a 3-4-2-1 with Acheampong back in a three, Neto at right wing-back and Estevão behind Rogers up front. Premier League News Now has a 4-2-3-1 with Gusto at right-back and Estevão on the right. Both bring Estevão in, which this projection does not.

What to watch

Martínez or Penders. Both are fit, and Alonso kept the choice to himself. It is the first sign of who he sees as his number one.

How many minutes Caicedo gets. He is cleared, but Ecuador managed his minutes over the break. If he comes off early, Henderson and Lavia are the obvious pair, with Barco next.

Who leads the line without João Pedro. Neto, Rogers pushed up, Welbeck or Emegha.
