This is a guess. The starting point is that Alonso named this exact eleven in the back-three shape last time out and has no obvious reason to break it up, so the projection is an unchanged side. Deadline day narrows his options rather than widening them: with Enzo Fernandez sold to Manchester City and the Lamine Camara deal dead, the central pair is Gusto alongside Lavia, and Moises Caicedo is now expected to be unavailable rather than merely doubtful, so there is almost no cover in there. Martinez keeps the goalkeeping job by default now that Robert Sanchez is on loan at Como. Reece James, Valentin Barco, Josh Acheampong, Estevao, Jamie Gittens and Danny Welbeck are the likeliest bench options, and James is the most credible name to force his way in, either at right centre-back or in midfield. The one thing most likely to break this projection is Caicedo: at least one injury tracker still has him as a major doubt rather than out, and if Alonso passes him fit on Friday he walks straight back into the middle, almost certainly in place of Gusto. Treat everything here as provisional until the team sheet lands about an hour before kickoff.

Both managers' pre-match press conferences are scheduled for Friday 4 September at 1:30pm UK and neither had taken place when this was written.

Alonso's first competitive meeting with childhood friend Mikel Arteta as a fellow manager. Arsenal are the reigning champions. This is Chelsea's first match since the summer transfer window shut on Tuesday 1 September, and it closed with two departures that change the team. Enzo Fernandez has gone to Manchester City for GBP 125m on a five-year deal, a British-record-equalling fee and a reunion with Enzo Maresca; Chelsea booked roughly an GBP 18m profit on the fee they paid Benfica three and a half years ago. Chelsea had lined up Monaco's Lamine Camara as the replacement and that deal collapsed late on deadline day, so there is no direct like-for-like signing to slot in. Goalkeeper Robert Sanchez also left, joining Cesc Fabregas's Como on a season-long loan, which leaves Emiliano Martinez and Mike Penders as the senior keepers. Marc Guiu moved to RB Leipzig. Availability: Moises Caicedo is now expected to miss out. He had already been carrying a knock, with Alonso saying only 'hopefully it wasn't more than last time' and that the club would assess him during the week; reporting as of 3 September has it as very unlikely he is available at the Emirates. That would make it three matches out of three missed for him. Worth being candid that the sources are not all equally strong on this: Sports Mole's 3 September list grades him a major doubt rather than a definite absentee, and Alonso himself called the substitution largely precautionary, so Friday's press conference is the moment this actually gets settled. Jordan Henderson is still out following wrist surgery; Alonso has given no return date, and Sports Mole projects him back around the Hull City match on 12 September rather than this one. Marco Palestra has yet to feature this season after a training injury and is being pencilled in for the same mid-September window. Both managers hold their pre-match press conferences on Friday 4 September at 1:30pm UK, which is 7:30am Central — the real team news on both sides lands that morning, so this page's projection is at its least reliable right now. Arsenal's own picture is unsettled, and it is concentrated at centre-back and in midfield. William Saliba is out with a back injury and on Sports Mole's projection is not expected back until around late November. Jurrien Timber has a groin problem with a target return of 12 September, so he should miss this one too. Cristhian Mosquera was withdrawn on 79 minutes against Aston Villa on Monday with a strain Arteta played down as precautionary, the player 'was feeling like he was about to have something', but he did not train at London Colney on Thursday 3 September and the sources genuinely disagree on whether he will be cleared; if he is not, Ezri Konsa, a GBP 51m summer signing from Villa, is the likely man in. Bruno Guimaraes, a GBP 75m arrival from Newcastle, is a major doubt: a groin issue kept him out of Arsenal's opener, he managed only 19 minutes as a substitute on Monday, and he was absent from Thursday's session as well. Declan Rice is a fresh doubt after limping off late on Monday, with no official word yet. Treat the Arsenal side as unsettled at the back and thinner than usual in midfield. Chelsea then host Leeds United in the Carabao Cup third round on the Wednesday, three days after this, so squad management is again in play.
