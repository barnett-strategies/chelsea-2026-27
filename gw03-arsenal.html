Alonso's first competitive meeting with childhood friend Mikel Arteta as a fellow manager. Arsenal are the reigning champions. This is Chelsea's first match since the summer transfer window shut on Tuesday 1 September, and it closed with two departures that change the team. Enzo Fernandez has gone to Manchester City for GBP 125m on a five-year deal, a British-record-equalling fee and a reunion with Enzo Maresca; Chelsea booked roughly an GBP 18m profit on the fee they paid Benfica three and a half years ago. Chelsea had lined up Monaco's Lamine Camara as the replacement and that deal collapsed late on deadline day, so there is no direct like-for-like signing to slot in; Alonso's public line on Friday was that he is 'prepared and happy with the squad and the depth we have'. Goalkeeper Robert Sanchez also left, joining Cesc Fabregas's Como on a season-long loan, which leaves Emiliano Martinez and Mike Penders as the senior keepers. Marc Guiu moved to RB Leipzig. CHELSEA: Moises Caicedo is NOT ruled out. Alonso deliberately gave nothing away, saying only 'We will check tomorrow. Marco Palestra did not come up at all, so he stays an unknown.
