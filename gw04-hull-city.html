Team news

Alonso has dropped both centre-backs this page expected to start. Maxence Lacroix and Josh Acheampong are both named among the substitutes — fit, available, and not selected. The week-long argument on this page about which of Acheampong and Fofana would take the right of the back three was the wrong argument: Wesley Fofana starts there, and the bigger call was Lacroix losing his place entirely.

The confirmed back three is Fofana, Colwill and Hato, with Malo Gusto coming into the side at wing-back. Gusto was not in a single predicted eleven published today.

Reece James captains the side and starts in midfield alongside Romeo Lavia, exactly as projected. Moisés Caicedo is out as Alonso confirmed on Friday, along with Jordan Henderson (wrist), Emmanuel Emegha (muscle) and Marco Palestra.

One loose end from Friday is now closed. This page flagged that previews disagreed on whether Valentín Barco was injured or a bench option. He is on the bench. The full nine: Penders, Acheampong, Lacroix, Chavarría, Barco, Quenda, Gittens, Estêvão, Welbeck.

Hull news from Friday stands. Paddy McNair and Brooke Norton-Cuffy were passed fit and Timothy Iroegbunam is available again. One late item: winger Sorba Thomas was involved in a road traffic accident near Hull's training ground on Friday. He was unharmed but was expected to be left out of the squad. Nobel Mendy's half-time withdrawal in midweek was planned rotation, not injury.

What changed from the projection

The projection got the spine right and the defence wrong. Eight of eleven are as projected: Martínez; Colwill; James and Lavia in midfield; Neto; Palmer, Rogers and João Pedro. The central call of the week — that Caicedo would be out and James would partner Lavia — is confirmed on the team sheet.

Three changes, all in defence. Fofana comes in for Acheampong at right centre-back, which settles the two-two source split this page flagged as genuinely open and resolves it against the name we were holding. Hato moves from left wing-back into the back three. And Gusto starts at wing-back in place of Lacroix, who drops out of the eleven altogether.

The Lacroix omission is the one nobody saw. All four predicted elevens tracked on this page started him, and so did every prediction read this morning. He is on the bench rather than injured, so this is a straight selection call by Alonso.

Being honest about the tie-break we used. Friday's note held Acheampong on recency, because Sports Mole was the only source refreshed after the press conference. That reasoning was sound and the answer was still wrong — and the actual change went further than either side of that debate, since the shape of the back three moved as well as the personnel. Kick-off, channel and odds were correct and are unchanged.

The tactical read

The shape is a 3-4-3, and the rebuild is entirely in the back four-plus-one. Alonso has gone with a taller, more experienced back three in Fofana, Colwill and Hato, and used Gusto as a specialist wing-back rather than picking a fourth centre-back.

The notable positional consequence is Pedro Neto switching flanks. Gusto is a natural right-back and is described as taking the right, which puts Neto on the left wing-back side — a right-footer on the left, able to cut inside onto his stronger foot against a packed box. Worth flagging that the sides here are inferred from Gusto's profile rather than stated on the sheet.

Hato into the back three is the possession call. Against a deep block the first pass has to break a line rather than beat a press, and moving a comfortable left-footed passer from wing-back into the back three gives Chelsea a cleaner angle to play through rather than around. It also means Chelsea have a genuinely two-footed back three rather than three right-siders.

The structural risk is unchanged and now live. Lavia starts a two-man midfield with Caicedo out and no recognised holding player on the bench — Barco and Quenda are the midfield-ish options behind him. His last two seasons were injury-hit.

Hull's record is the reason all this care is being taken. They arrive unbeaten on seven points, the only side in the league yet to concede, and Jakirovic has said plainly they will stay compact and defend as a unit rather than man-mark. The market prices Chelsea at a de-vigged 78 percent.

What to watch

Which flank Pedro Neto actually takes. With Gusto in at wing-back, Neto looks to have switched to the left. Whether that holds, or whether the two swap sides in possession, is the first thing the shape will tell you.

Whether the rebuilt back three can play through a block. Fofana, Colwill and Hato is a more press-resistant trio than the projected one. Hull will sit deep and invite the spare centre-back to carry — watch who accepts that invitation and how far forward they are allowed to go.

Romeo Lavia's workload. Two-man midfield, no like-for-like cover on the bench. Watch how long he is given and whether Chelsea reshape rather than replace him.
