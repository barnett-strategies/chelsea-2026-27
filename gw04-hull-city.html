This is the traditional Saturday 3pm UK slot, which means it is blacked out on UK television, but American viewers get it on linear TV: NBC Sports lists the fixture as 10am ET on USA Network, which is 9am Central. That listing comes from NBC's own published matchweek schedule rather than a third-party guide, and it agrees with the 3pm UK time quoted independently by Goal, so both the time and the channel can be treated as firm. Worth flagging that USA Network is the good outcome here - four of the other five 10am kick-offs that morning are Peacock-only. The market is pricing a full-strength Chelsea here and a rotated one on Wednesday, which is a useful independent check on the heavy-rotation expectation recorded on the cup page. TEAM NEWS SO FAR, which is thin because the Leeds cup tie on 9 September is soaking up all the reporting: Moises Caicedo is being openly targeted at this match rather than the cup tie. He has not played since lasting 14 minutes against Brighton, and Alonso has said the injury is not longer-term but that he will not repeat the mistake of rushing him back, so Hull is the stated return date rather than Wednesday. Marco Palestra is also pointed at this game, having missed all four matches so far with a training-ground knock. Jordan Henderson is still waiting on his Chelsea debut with the wrist injury he carried out of the World Cup - he was pictured in first-team training on 4 September and Alonso is waiting on a green light, so mid-to-late September looks more realistic than this weekend.
