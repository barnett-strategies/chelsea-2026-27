Team news

Moisés Caicedo is the one genuinely open question, and it resolves Friday. He is listed as a major doubt with the problem specified as a recurrence of the calf injury picked up against Brighton, which is why he missed both the Arsenal league trip and the midweek cup tie. Reporting now has him close to returning but describes the club as deliberately cautious about a second setback, with a late call set for Friday. At least one outlet has already moved his realistic return to 18 September against Brentford. He has played 14 minutes all season, so even a green light is unlikely to mean ninety.

Three players are out, and Palestra sits alongside Caicedo on the same late call. Jordan Henderson (wrist, carried out of the World Cup) is now pointed at Brentford on 18 September, so his Chelsea debut waits again. Emmanuel Emegha is out with no return date and a Friday review. Marco Palestra has still not featured this season after a training-ground knock; the club is unwilling to rush him despite his own optimism, and a fortnight of reporting pointing him at this match has not turned into anything confirmed. Chelsea have no suspensions.

Levi Colwill is fit, and Alonso has now said so twice. A fitness question raised over him in midweek was dismissed directly: No, no. It is just from little things that he's having, but we don't expect that it's an injury. He is expected to start in the back three rather than merely be available.

Wesley Fofana is fit too, which matters for how his omission should be read. He is named as available defensive cover on the bench, not as a casualty. No injury or fitness problem is reported for him anywhere. The unsourced claim that Chelsea would be without Emiliano Martínez, checked and rejected on this page twice now, has still produced no supporting report and he remains projected to start.

The tactical read

The shape is unchanged: Alonso's settled 3-4-2-1 - back three, two high wing-backs, a midfield pair, two free eights behind a lone striker. Both prediction sources write it as a 3-4-3, which is the same side described differently, with Palmer and Rogers as forwards rather than as eights.

The midfield pair is now corroborated rather than assumed. Reporting drawn from Alonso's own comments has James, Lavia and a fit Caicedo as the three candidates for two places, which means that with Caicedo a major doubt the pair picks itself: Reece James alongside Romeo Lavia. Neto therefore stays at right wing-back and Hato holds the left, so a winger and a converted defender supply the width.

The real tactical problem here is much sharper than a generic low block, and this is the most important new fact on the page. Hull City have not conceded a single league goal this season - three clean sheets in three matches, unbeaten, seven points, and only the third promoted side in Premier League history to open a top-flight campaign that way. Sergej Jakirovic's side is a well-drilled defensive unit that has already frustrated established opposition, not a soft landing for a rotated favourite.

That reframes what the setup has to do. Against a side yet to be broken down, the burden falls on patience and on the quality of service from wide, and Colwill's presence on the left of the three earns its keep in possession as much as out of it - the first pass has to break a settled low line rather than beat a press. The load question remains Lavia, whose season has been injury-hit and who has no recognised holding partner in reserve if Caicedo is not passed fit.

Whether Hull's clean sheet finally goes, and how long it takes. They arrive as the only side in the league yet to concede. The market has Chelsea at a de-vigged 78 percent, which prices a comfortable afternoon, so the gap between that number and Hull's defensive record is the most interesting tension in the fixture. Watch the first fifteen minutes: whether Chelsea probe patiently or force it early.

Colwill's first league start of the season, and how he is managed. He played four times in all of last season coming back from an ACL injury and has now had a fitness question raised and waved away inside a week. Watch whether he lasts the full match or comes off as a precaution.
