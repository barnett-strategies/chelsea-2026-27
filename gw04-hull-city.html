Team news

Moisés Caicedo is the one name that decides this XI, and he is still genuinely undecided. Alonso was asked directly whether Caicedo could return here and said potentially yes, but that they need to wait and would probably know more Thursday or Friday. That is a first-hand quote, not a paraphrase, and it reads exactly as it sounds: open, leaning positive, resolved later this week. He has played 14 minutes all season, having lasted a quarter of an hour against Brighton before coming off.

Three other names sit on that same Thursday-Friday review. Jordan Henderson is still chasing his Chelsea debut with the wrist injury he carried out of the World Cup; he was in first-team training on 4 September but mid-to-late September remains more realistic than this weekend. Emmanuel Emegha is on the same review. Marco Palestra has missed every match so far with a training-ground knock and this game is his stated target, with reporting framing Saturday as far likelier for him than midweek — but nothing about him is confirmed.

Levi Colwill is fit and available, which matters here mostly as a negative: he is not a new doubt being carried into the weekend.

One circulating claim was checked and deliberately not recorded — a search summary asserting Chelsea are without Emiliano Martínez for this match. Nothing supports it. The same site's own matchweek-4 page starts him, and no injury or suspension report exists. Treated as unsourced and ignored; he is projected to start.

The tactical read

The shape is Alonso's settled 3-4-2-1 — a back three, two wing-backs pushed high, a midfield pair, and two free eights behind a lone striker. It is what Chelsea used at Arsenal and it is what the corroborating prediction expects here.

The most Alonso-specific detail is Reece James in central midfield rather than at right-back. The club captain has been used in the midfield two, which pushes Neto — a winger by trade — into the right wing-back berth and leaves Hato to hold the left. Against a Hull side expected to defend deep in a five, those two wing-backs are the width, and James becomes the long-range distributor from a central platform rather than a crosser from the touchline.

The back three is the softest part of this projection. Acheampong and Lacroix have now played the Arsenal league match and the midweek cup tie back to back, so Saturday would be a third game in seven days for both. Fofana was rested in midweek and looks the safest name of the three; Colwill is fit and is the obvious alternative if either of the other two is managed. Do not read the defence as settled.

If Caicedo is passed fit, the natural move is him into the midfield two, which most likely releases James back toward defence or the bench rather than displacing Lavia.

What to watch

Whether Caicedo plays at all, and in what role. The Thursday-or-Friday call is the single most consequential piece of news still outstanding for this match. Fourteen minutes of football all season means even a green light probably does not mean ninety.

Whether Palestra finally debuts. He has missed every match so far and this game has been his target for a fortnight. A right wing-back arriving would let Neto move forward and change the look of the whole right side.

How Chelsea handle a deep-lying five. Hull are expected to sit in with a back five and a four in front of it. At a de-vigged 78 percent, the market is pricing a full-strength Chelsea comfortably breaking that down — patience and the quality of service from the wing-backs are the things to watch, rather than any early flurry.
