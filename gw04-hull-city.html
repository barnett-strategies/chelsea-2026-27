Hull City return to the Premier League as Championship play-off winners. KICK-OFF AND US CHANNEL ARE NOW CONFIRMED (checked Tuesday 8 September). This is the traditional Saturday 3pm UK slot, which means it is blacked out on UK television, but American viewers get it on linear TV: NBC Sports lists the fixture as 10am ET on USA Network, which is 9am Central. That listing comes from NBC's own published matchweek schedule rather than a third-party guide, and it agrees with the 3pm UK time quoted independently by Goal, so both the time and the channel can be treated as firm. Worth flagging that USA Network is the good outcome here - four of the other five 10am kick-offs that morning are Peacock-only. ODDS ARE STILL NOT RECORDED, and this is a deliberate omission rather than an oversight. Every mainstream book and odds-comparison site refused the request on 8 September: FanDuel, DraftKings, Oddschecker, Oddspedia, Forebet, BetUS and YesPlay all returned 403. The only two numbers that surfaced came through search summaries that could not be opened and verified - YesPlay quoting 1.23 on Chelsea, 6.60 the draw and 11.00 on Hull, and Polymarket, a prediction market rather than a sportsbook, trading Chelsea 79 cents, the draw 15 and Hull 7. Those two agree closely once normalised, at roughly 77-79 percent Chelsea, 14-15 percent draw and 7-9 percent Hull, so the shape of the market is clear enough: a heavy home favourite, materially shorter than the Leeds cup price. But neither source is a mainstream sportsbook and neither could be read first-hand, so nothing has been written into the odds fields. This should be picked up on the next run, when the briefing text falls due. TEAM NEWS SO FAR, which is thin because the Leeds cup tie on 9 September is soaking up all the reporting: Moises Caicedo is being openly targeted at this match rather than the cup tie. He has not played since lasting 14 minutes against Brighton, and Alonso has said the injury is not longer-term but that he will not repeat the mistake of rushing him back, so Hull is the stated return date rather than Wednesday. Marco Palestra is also pointed at this game, having missed all four matches so far with a training-ground knock. Jordan Henderson is still waiting on his Chelsea debut with the wrist injury he carried out of the World Cup - he was pictured in first-team training on 4 September and Alonso is waiting on a green light, so mid-to-late September looks more realistic than this weekend. NO PROJECTED XI YET, on purpose. Nothing current exists: the only Chelsea-versus-Hull predicted lineup in circulation is dated 13 February 2026 and is for an FA Cup tie at the MKM Stadium under a different manager, with several players in it who are now unavailable or gone. It was discarded. Any XI for this match is also hostage to how heavily Alonso rotates on Wednesday, so a projection is worth more after the cup tie than before it.
