A guess, and one built on continuity rather than team news — but an unusually tidy guess, because Goal and FotMob arrive at the identical 3-4-2-1 from different directions: Sánchez behind an Acheampong-Lacroix-Colwill back three, Gusto and Hato as the wing-backs, Reece James pushed forward into a midfield two with Roméo Lavia, and Palmer plus Morgan Rogers working off João Pedro. The least settled call is that midfield: Moisés Caicedo and Marco Palestra both missed the opening matchday squad with minor training knocks and are expected back around Thursday's EFL Cup tie against Luton, and if Caicedo is passed fit he is very hard to leave out — which would most likely send James back into the back three and squeeze either Lavia or Acheampong out. Enzo Fernández is available but not in the modal XI, and Alonso's own explanation is the reason to take that seriously: he has said his players came through pre-season at different stages and that he is managing minutes accordingly, with a Thursday cup tie three days before this one. Wesley Fofana's ban is the one thing that got clearer rather than murkier — the length flagged as uncertain before Craven Cottage has resolved as two matches, so he misses this as well; Jordan Henderson (wrist, sustained at the World Cup) is not expected back until September, Emanuel Emegha (hamstring) is back in training with this fixture named as a possible return, and Caleb Wiley, Aaron Anselmino and Mamadou Sarr are variously listed out or doubtful. One discrepancy worth naming: FotMob's auto-generated availability panel still flags Palmer as a doubt, but Alonso raised no concern about him after the opening weekend and every preview puts him in the XI, so treat that flag as stale.
