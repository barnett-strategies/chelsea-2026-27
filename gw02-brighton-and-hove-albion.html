Still a guess, and the guess itself has not moved: Goal's 25 August preview and FotMob arrive at the identical 3-4-2-1 from different directions — Sánchez behind an Acheampong-Lacroix-Colwill back three, Gusto and Hato as the wing-backs, Reece James pushed forward into a midfield two with Roméo Lavia, and Palmer plus Morgan Rogers working off João Pedro. What moved on 26 August is the availability picture around it, and one item is a straight correction to what this page said earlier: Wesley Fofana is available for this match, not banned for it. His two-game suspension, carried over from the final-day red card at Sunderland, is discharged by the Fulham opener plus Thursday's EFL Cup tie against Luton, because a sending-off ban is served across domestic competitions rather than in the Premier League alone — Rotowire and Sports Mole both have him clear for Sunday. He is a rotation option for the back three rather than a projected starter, since the previews that build this XI still start Colwill. Alonso's Wednesday press conference sharpened the midfield question without settling it: Moisés Caicedo and Marco Palestra are both out of the Luton tie, and the phrasing was pointedly uneven — “Both not for tomorrow. Hopefully Moi can make it for the weekend. Maybe we need to check a little bit more with Marco.” So Caicedo is a real chance here and Palestra is not, and if Caicedo is passed fit he is very hard to leave out, which would most likely send James back into the back three and squeeze either Lavia or Acheampong out. Enzo Fernández is fit, has trained well and will be involved against Luton, but his is the live situation: Manchester City are readying a fresh bid with the window closing at 11pm BST on Tuesday 1 September — two days after this match, so he should be selectable — and Alonso has said he wants the future resolved this week. Cole Palmer is fine; Alonso reported no fresh concern, which retires the stale doubt flag FotMob's automated panel was still showing. Jordan Henderson (wrist, sustained at the World Cup) is now given a target of 6 September at Arsenal, Emanuel Emegha is still listed out through injury, Aaron Anselmino (hamstring) is a minor doubt, and Caleb Wiley and Mamadou Sarr remain variously out or doubtful.
