Team news

Alonso cleared all five headline returners at Friday's press conference. Moisés Caicedo, João Pedro, Reece James, Cole Palmer and Marco Palestra have trained and are ready to go, in his words. On Caicedo: "He's been training good and he's okay to go."

Palmer was not rushed. Alonso said he trained all last week and felt better, and that the priority was for him to be fully fit. That reads as available, not necessarily 90 minutes.

Jamie Gittens is the one confirmed absence. He hurt his ankle with England Under-21s and Alonso said he will be out for a few weeks.

Nothing new was said about Lavia, Rogers, Hato, Neto, Penders or Emegha. Hato, Neto and Penders were back in training on 7 October, and Lavia and Rogers came through international duty, so all are treated as available.

What changed from the projection

Four changes from the projection this page carried since 6 October, all driven by the press conference. João Pedro replaces Welbeck up front, Caicedo replaces Lavia in midfield, Palestra replaces Neto at right wing-back and Hato replaces Chavarría at left wing-back.

What held up: the 3-4-2-1, the back three of Fofana, Lacroix and Colwill, Martínez in goal, James in midfield and Palmer with Rogers behind the striker.

The earlier caution on João Pedro and Caicedo was reasonable but is now out of date. Both were doubts on the 1 and 5 October reports; Alonso has since declared both ready.

The tactical read

Still the 3-4-2-1, but with Chelsea's first-choice spine restored. Caicedo and James give Alonso the central pair he has not had all season, and João Pedro returns as a genuine centre-forward in place of a stand-in.

The wing-backs are the softest part of this guess. Palestra and Hato follow Football.London's prediction, but Neto and Chavarría started the projection for good reason and either could easily keep his place.

Minutes management is the wild card. Caicedo, João Pedro and Palmer have all had little or no football recently, so early substitutions, or one of them starting on the bench, would not be a surprise.

What to watch

Whether João Pedro starts or is held back. Alonso has cleared him, but Football.London notes he may be cautious after the knee injury. Welbeck is the fallback.

Caicedo and James together. If both start, it is the first time this season Chelsea field their intended midfield pairing.

The wing-back picks. Palestra and Hato against Neto and Chavarría is the call most likely to differ from this projection. The official team sheet is due at 1:45 PM UK, 7:45 AM CT.
