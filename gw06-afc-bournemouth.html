Team news

Six of Chelsea's eight absentees are targeting this match for their return. A Yahoo Sports injury table dated 1 October lists João Pedro (knee), Cole Palmer (muscle), Moisés Caicedo (calf), Reece James (hamstring), Marco Palestra (quad), Emmanuel Emegha (hamstring) and Mike Penders (quad) all with a 10 October target. Targets are not availability, and Chelsea will assess everyone in the final days of the week.

Palmer withdrew from the England squad with a muscle problem and has been working with a rehab coach at Cobham. A 5 October report says he and Palestra are expected to be fit for selection, and that James is expected back after what was thought to be a minor issue.

Caicedo is the one going the wrong way. That same 5 October report calls him increasingly unlikely to start and possibly not in the squad. He has managed only minutes this season. Jamie Gittens has an ankle injury from England Under-21 duty, was seen on crutches and has no return date. Romeo Lavia played for Belgium during the break and came off late with cramp only.

Four players were back in training at Cobham on 7 October: Mike Penders, Jorrel Hato, Estêvão and Pedro Neto. Rogers and Lavia were due back from international duty shortly after. The same report still lists James, Caicedo, João Pedro and Palmer as uncertain, pending Alonso's Friday update.

João Pedro is the open question. He withdrew from the Brazil squad and the 1 October table targets this match. A separate, undated report says late October, so this page treats him as a doubt rather than a starter.

The tactical read

The same 3-4-2-1 Alonso has used all season, with the returners slotted back into their usual places. Fofana, Lacroix and Colwill stay as the back three. Neto and Chavarría stay at wing-back, and Palmer and Rogers sit behind a lone striker.

The midfield is where the returns matter most. Last time out Alonso had to move Valentín Barco, a natural left-back, into the centre next to Henderson because he had run out of midfielders. With James back and Lavia fit, Chelsea have a recognised central pair again. That is the biggest structural improvement on offer this week.

The striker slot is the least secure call on this page. If João Pedro passes his fitness checks he goes straight in. Until there is evidence that he will, Welbeck is the projection.

What to watch

João Pedro's fitness. He is the difference between a recognised centre-forward and a stand-in. Watch Alonso's Friday press conference (1:30 PM UK, 7:30 AM CT) for the steer.

Whether Palmer starts or is eased in. He has just pulled out of an international squad with a muscle problem, so a place on the bench would not be a surprise.

James back in midfield. If he starts there alongside Lavia, Chelsea finally have the central pairing their projections have been built around.
