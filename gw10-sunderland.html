Team news

Caicedo passed his Sunday check and starts. The discomfort Alonso described on Friday has not kept him out.

Emi Martínez is not in the matchday squad. Mike Penders keeps the gloves, with Teddy Sharman-Lowe as the back-up goalkeeper.

Josh Acheampong is not in the matchday squad. He was in the projected back three, and no reason has been given yet. Estêvão drops to the bench.

Bench: Emegha, Gusto, Quenda, Anselmino, Nicoll-Jazuli, Welbeck, Barco, Estêvão, Sharman-Lowe. Anselmino is in a matchday squad for the first time this season.

What changed from the projection

The shape is the big miss. We projected a back three; Alonso has gone to a back four, with Palestra at right-back and Pep Chavarría in at left-back.

Henderson starts alongside Caicedo and Lavia, not instead of one of them, so the midfield is a three. Estêvão and Acheampong are the two who drop out of the projected eleven.

What we got right: Penders in goal, Caicedo fit, Fofana and Colwill at centre-back, and Palmer and Rogers in the attacking roles. Nine of the eleven names were correct.

The tactical read

ESPN lists it as a 4-3-2-1; WhoScored has it as a 4-2-3-1. In both versions Pedro Neto leads the line instead of Palmer playing as a false nine.

Neto moves from wing-back to centre-forward, so his speed is now aimed at the space behind Sunderland's centre-backs. Palmer and Rogers play just behind him.

Three central midfielders suggest Alonso wants control and protection away from home, with Henderson's experience next to Caicedo and Lavia.

What to watch

Neto as a striker. It is a new role for him. Watch whether he runs in behind or drops deep to link play.

Chavarría and Palestra as full-backs. With no wing-backs, watch how high they push and who covers behind them.

Caicedo's fitness. He was a doubt until this morning. Watch whether he lasts the full ninety minutes.
