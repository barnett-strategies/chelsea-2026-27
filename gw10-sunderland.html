Team news

Moisés Caicedo is a genuine doubt. Alonso said on Friday that Caicedo had "little problems" after the last game, describing it as "a discomfort, more than the calf." He will be checked on Saturday and the final call comes on Sunday.

Emi Martínez could return. He is back in training in protective headgear after jaw surgery and is ahead of schedule. Alonso: "Tomorrow we will check, Sunday final decision."

Jorrel Hato is out. Alonso: "He is not fit yet. It will take some more weeks." João Pedro (thigh), Reece James (knee), Maxence Lacroix (hamstring) and Jamie Gittens (ankle) are also out.

Jordan Henderson is available again after illness. Aaron Anselmino is back in training but has not played this season and is listed as doubtful.

What changed from the projection

The projection still repeats the eleven Alonso picked against Manchester United, Caicedo included. Alonso has not ruled him out, and the call will not be made until Sunday, so he stays in for now. Treat that pick as a coin flip.

If Caicedo misses out, Henderson is the likeliest replacement. He is fit again after illness, so the midfield would become Henderson and Lavia. Henderson could also replace Lavia even if Caicedo plays.

Penders stays in goal in the projection. Martínez is ahead of schedule, but a Sunday decision on a player returning from jaw surgery points to Penders starting and Martínez on the bench at most.

The tactical read

We expect a 3-4-2-1 again. The back three would be Acheampong, Fofana and Colwill. Palestra and Neto would play as wing-backs either side of Caicedo and Lavia.

Palmer would play as a false nine while João Pedro is out, with Estêvão and Rogers just behind him. With no fit senior striker in the projection, Chelsea's attack depends on movement rather than a fixed target man.

What to watch

Whether Caicedo starts. Alonso called it a discomfort, not an injury, and left the decision until matchday. If he is missing, Chelsea lose their best tackler on a ground where they could be under pressure.

Who starts in goal. If Martínez plays in a mask this soon after jaw surgery, that shows how stretched Alonso thinks the squad is.

The Europa League hangover. Sunderland play in Europe on Thursday. Watch whether their legs go in the second half.
