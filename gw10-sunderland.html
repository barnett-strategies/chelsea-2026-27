Team news

João Pedro is out with a thigh injury and is expected back after the November international break. Reece James (knee) and Maxence Lacroix (hamstring) are both out until around January. Jamie Gittens (ankle) and Aaron Anselmino are also unavailable.

Three doubts could still be fit. Emi Martínez is recovering from a broken jaw, and Alonso says there is a chance he plays in a protective mask. Jorrel Hato has a back problem. Jordan Henderson missed the Manchester United match with illness, not an injury, and is expected back.

What changed from the projection

This is the first projection for this match. It repeats the eleven Alonso picked against Manchester United, because no new injury has been reported for any of those players.

The most likely changes are in goal and midfield. If Martínez is cleared, he would take back the gloves from Penders. Henderson could return in place of Lavia.

The tactical read

We expect a 3-4-2-1 again. The back three would be Acheampong, Fofana and Colwill. Palestra and Neto would play as wing-backs either side of Caicedo and Lavia.

Palmer would play as a false nine while João Pedro is out, with Estêvão and Rogers just behind him. With no fit senior striker in the projection, Chelsea's attack depends on movement rather than a fixed target man.

What to watch

Who starts in goal. If Martínez plays in a mask this soon after a broken jaw, that shows how stretched Alonso thinks the squad is.

The Europa League hangover. Sunderland play in Europe on Thursday. Watch whether their legs go in the second half.

Henderson or Lavia. Henderson would give a calmer, slower midfield. Lavia gives one that presses harder.
