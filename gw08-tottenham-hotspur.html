Team news

Reece James is out until around the new year after knee surgery. Emegha and Gittens were also out for Everton, and there is no fresh word on either yet.

Fofana, Palestra and Lavia were all on the bench last weekend, so all three are fit and in contention. Pep Chavarria missed that matchday squad entirely, for a reason that hasn't been reported.

Alonso's pre-Spurs press conference should land Thursday or Friday. This page will be updated from it.
