Team news

Emiliano Martínez is out after jaw surgery, and Alonso said of Mike Penders: "He is ready." Penders is projected to make his first Premier League start in goal.

Pep Chavarría is ruled out. Alonso: "Pep is training a little bit more but he is not going to make it." Emmanuel Emegha also misses out, and Reece James (knee, around mid-January) and Jamie Gittens (ankle) are out long term.

Malo Gusto is fit. "Malo is fine," Alonso said, which gives him another right-sided option. Fofana, Palestra and Lavia were on the bench last weekend, so all three are fit.

This is Alonso's Friday press conference, per talkchelsea (23 Oct) and AOL. The official team sheet will settle it on Saturday.

The tactical read

Projected as the same back three with one forced change: Penders in goal behind Acheampong, Lacroix and Colwill, with Neto and Hato at wing-back, Caicedo and Henderson in midfield, and Palmer and Rogers behind João Pedro.

This is a guess built from the last confirmed team sheet plus the goalkeeper news, not from any steer from Alonso. The likeliest further changes are Fofana for Acheampong, Palestra for Neto, or Lavia for Henderson. Gusto's return adds a fourth option at right wing-back or on the right of the back three.

What to watch

Penders in a derby. A 21-year-old's first Premier League start comes against Spurs at the Bridge, so watch how he handles crosses and set pieces.

Who partners Caicedo. Henderson got the nod at Everton. A second straight start would say he is Alonso's answer while James is out.

Neto's defensive shift at right wing-back in a derby. The question is whether Alonso keeps the attacking option or goes back to Palestra.
