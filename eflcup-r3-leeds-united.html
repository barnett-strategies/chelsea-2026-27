An all-Premier League tie at Stamford Bridge, Chelsea's third home match in under three weeks. Leeds are managed by Daniel Farke and are back in the third round for the first time since 2022/23. Date and kick-off are confirmed by Chelsea: Wednesday 9 September, 8pm UK, which is 2pm Central. UK coverage is Sky Sports Main Event. For US viewers the important point is that this is NOT an NBC competition - CBS and Paramount Plus hold the American rights to the Carabao Cup, and Paramount Plus streams the large majority of ties in every round, which is also where the second-round tie was carried. The per-match US listing had still not been published as of 5 September, so treat the platform as reliable and a specific channel number as unconfirmed. No mainstream sportsbook had priced this tie as of 5 September either. The only numbers circulating are tipster model projections, which are not odds and are not recorded here, so the odds fields are deliberately still empty and will be filled once a real book posts a line - most likely once the Arsenal match is out of the way. There is also no basis yet for a projected XI: Alonso's pre-match press conference does not happen until early in the week. What can be said is that the tie falls three days after the Arsenal away trip and four days before Hull at home, and Alonso rotated heavily in the second round, so a much-changed side should be the expectation rather than a surprise.
