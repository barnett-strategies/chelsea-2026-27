An all-Premier League tie at Stamford Bridge, Chelsea's third home match in under three weeks. Leeds are managed by Daniel Farke and are back in the third round for the first time since 2022/23. Date and kick-off are confirmed by Chelsea: Wednesday 9 September, 8pm UK, which is 2pm Central. UK coverage is Sky Sports Main Event. For US viewers the important point is that this is NOT an NBC competition - CBS and Paramount Plus hold the American rights to the Carabao Cup, and Paramount Plus streams the large majority of ties in every round, which is also where the second-round tie was carried.
